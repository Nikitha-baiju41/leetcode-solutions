2889. Reshape Data: Pivot
File: 2889_reshape_data_pivot.py

def pivotTable(weather: pd.DataFrame) -> pd.DataFrame:
    return weather.pivot(
        index="month",
        columns="city",
        values="temperature"
    ).reset_index().rename_axis(columns=None)

Note:
reset_index() makes "month" a regular output column, as required by
LeetCode's expected result. The numeric values shown at the far left
(0, 1, 2, ...) are only the DataFrame row index, not an extra column.
Do not remove reset_index() for the LeetCode submission, or "month"
will remain the index instead of a regular column.
